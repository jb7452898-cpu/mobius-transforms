How to read the picture

α and β each have a real and an imaginary part. A bar means complex conjugation: change the sign of the imaginary part. Normalization divides all four entered components by their combined length.

Every point z on the left is sent to f(z) on the right. A denominator of zero sends a point to infinity. Curves are split near that pole so they do not draw false connections across the graph. Some transformed points fall outside the visible area. The source grid is sampled over a finite region; zooming changes that region.

This normalized family corresponds to sphere rotations under stereographic projection. The sphere below shows the same transformation as a rotation. The viewing angle can be changed independently by dragging; this does not change α or β.
